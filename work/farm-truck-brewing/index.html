The work

Make the digital presence feel as alive as the taproom.

Approach

The work starts with what is genuinely happening: new beer, live music, local collaborations and the people behind the bar. Each piece of content needs to communicate clearly without sanding away the brewery’s character.

01

Show the place honestly.

Use real photography and video from the brewery, preserving the atmosphere instead of manufacturing one.

02

Build a useful rhythm.

Turn releases, events and weekly programming into a repeatable system people can recognize and act on.

03

Keep the brand coherent.

Give every format its own purpose while keeping photography, type and messaging unmistakably Farm Truck.

Project scope

From the weekly calendar to the finished post.

  • Photography
  • Short-form video
  • Campaign design
  • Social media direction
